ATHANG CHESS Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

ATHANG CHESS ("we", "our", or "the app") is a chess training application. This policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the application.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled in the app, information may include:

  • Account details, such as an email address and authentication identifier.
  • Account registration and login information needed to manage access.
  • Chess activity, saved games, training progress, or preferences, if these features store data.
  • Technical diagnostics and crash reports, if collected by enabled services.
  • Purchase and subscription status, if paid features are offered.

The actual information processed depends on the features you use and the third-party services integrated into the installed version of the app.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Create accounts and authenticate users.
  • Provide chess training and app functionality.
  • Maintain access to eligible paid features.
  • Protect the app and accounts against misuse.
  • Diagnose errors and maintain app reliability.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

4. Firebase and Other Service Providers

ATHANG CHESS may use Google Firebase services for authentication, data storage, analytics, or diagnostics, depending on which services are enabled. These providers may process relevant information to deliver their services.

Any other integrated services, including payment providers, may process information necessary for their functions. The final policy must reflect the services actually used by the app.

5. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the app, when necessary for security, when required by law, or with the user's consent where applicable.

We do not sell personal information. This statement does not remove the need to disclose any data sharing performed by integrated services.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

To request account deletion or ask a privacy question, contact the developer using the support email provided on the app's Google Play listing. Include the account email needed to identify your account, but do not send your password.

Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements. Any applicable retention exceptions will be explained to the user.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

ATHANG CHESS must follow the age and audience requirements declared for the app on Google Play. Any personal information relating to children will be handled in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play policies.

9. Third-Party Policies

Third-party services may process information under their own privacy policies. For information about Google services, see Google Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this webpage with an updated effective date where appropriate.
